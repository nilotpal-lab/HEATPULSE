# Synthetic Health Demo Warning

`synthetic-health-demo.csv` and `synthetic-health-demo-model.json` are synthetic
pipeline fixtures created to unblock software development. Their mortality and
hospitalization event columns are generated labels, not observed health records.

The artifacts are not evidence, are not calibrated to India, and must not be used
for clinical decisions, public-health alerts, mortality estimates, hospitalization
estimates, or production HeatPulse outputs.
